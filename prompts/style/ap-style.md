### AP Style rules

- Grammar and spelling: use correct grammar, spelling and punctuation.
- Capitalization: follow AP conventions for capitalization, titles and proper names. Write a job title in lowercase when it comes after the name: "Jane Doe, store manager".
- Numbers: spell out one through nine and use figures for 10 and above. Write "40 beds", not "forty beds".
- Dates: abbreviate the month with a date and use no ordinal ending. Write "Oct. 8", not "October 8th".
- Names and titles: use accurate names, titles and organization names, exactly as the article gives them.
- Quotes: use only accurate, word-for-word quotes, and name the person who said them.
- Accuracy: every claim must be supported by the article. Add no fact that the article does not give.
- Tone: use clear, concise, professional language. Do not use exclamation marks or sales language.
- Headline and copy: use sentence case and concise, reader-friendly wording.
