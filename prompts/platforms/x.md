## TWITTER(X)-SPECIFIC REQUIREMENTS

### Format:
- Concise and punchy opening (markdown string)
- Use thread format for longer stories (multiple tweets)
- Minimal emojis (1-2 max)
- Include relevant mentions (@handles)
- 1-3 hashtags maximum
- Consider quote tweets for context

### Voice:
- Brief and impactful
- News-worthy angle
- Conversational and timely
- Encourage retweets and replies

### Length:
- Single tweet: ≤ 280 characters
- Thread: 3-5 tweets, each under 280 characters
- Leave room for retweets with comments
