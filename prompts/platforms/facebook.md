## FACEBOOK-SPECIFIC REQUIREMENTS

### Format:
- One short paragraph of plain text. No line breaks, no lists and no markdown.
- At most ${platformRules.facebook.characterLimit} characters, with spaces.
- No hashtags. Return an empty "hashtags" array.
- No emojis.
- Do not write a link or a URL. The app adds the link to the article.
- Do not end with a question or a call to comment.

### Voice:
- Write in the third person. Use the name of the advertiser, ${organization.legal_name}. Never use "we", "our" or "us".
- The first time that you name the advertiser, write it as @${organization.facebook_handle} and not as the plain name.
- Write the names of partner organizations as plain names.

### Sources:
- Take every claim from the story article. Add no fact that the article does not give.
- Use a quote only if the article has it, word for word, with the name and the job title of the speaker. Never invent a quote.
- Do not copy text from Spokesman news or editorial stories.
