## INSTAGRAM-SPECIFIC REQUIREMENTS

### Format:
- emotionally engaging, CTA-driven opening line (markdown string)
- 2-3 short paragraphs with line breaks
- Use emojis generously (3-5, relevant to content)
- Strong call-to-action
- 5-10 hashtags (mix of popular and niche)
- Consider Instagram Stories format for behind-the-scenes

### Voice:
- Casual and authentic
- Visual storytelling focus
- Emotional and relatable
- Community-focused language

### Length:
- 120-150 words for feed posts
- Shorter for Stories (50-75 words)
